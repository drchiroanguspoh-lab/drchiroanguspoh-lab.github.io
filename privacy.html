Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Who we are

This website and the "BP CHiRO Shorts" app are run by Dr Angus Poh for the YouTube channel "Dr Angus | Clinic Growth" and the Instagram account @anguspoh.bpchiro. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What the app does

"BP CHiRO Shorts" is a private tool used only by Dr Angus Poh to upload his own short videos to his own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

Google user data the app accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): used only to upload Dr Angus Poh's own videos, with their titles and descriptions, to his own channel.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly): used only to confirm which channel is connected.

The app does not read, collect or store any data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on Dr Angus Poh's own computer, in a file that only his user account can read. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train any AI or machine learning model.

Limited use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes the app's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @anguspoh.bpchiro on Instagram.
